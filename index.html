How this works

Where the numbers come from
  • Community submissions are anonymous entries from the form. A cohort (role + level + country) needs at least 5 of them before its real median is published — below that, one person's salary would be identifiable.
  • Indicative ranges are estimates compiled from public recruiter salary guides and community reports for 2025–2026. They are a placeholder, clearly labelled, and are replaced by real data as submissions arrive. Do not quote them as fact.
What "total package" means

Gulf offers split pay into base plus allowances, which makes headline numbers misleading. Everything here is the monthly total cash package:

  • Base salary
  • Housing, transport and any other recurring cash allowance
  • Annual bonus, divided by 12

It excludes equity, relocation one-offs, school fees and medical cover — those vary too much to average honestly.

Privacy
  • No names, no emails, no login. The form does not collect identity.
  • Company name is optional and never shown on a row — only company type is published.
  • Nationality is optional. It is only ever shown as an aggregate over at least 8 people, never on an individual row.
  • Submissions are moderated before publication to strip anything identifying.
Currency conversion

GCC currencies are pegged to the US dollar, so conversion uses fixed rates rather than a live feed: AED 3.6725, SAR 3.75, QAR 3.64, KWD 0.3065, BHD 0.376, OMR 0.3845 per USD.

The data is only as good as what people share
